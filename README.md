# 🛒 Online Retail Orders – SQL Mini Project

## 📖 What Happens After a Customer Clicks "Buy Now"?

Every click on “Buy Now” sets off a chain reaction: an order is placed, inventory is updated, and the payment is processed. But behind this smooth shopping experience lies a complex web of data—waiting to be analyzed.

In this project, we dive deep into an online retail database that captures every piece of the customer journey. From orders and items to customer profiles and payments, our goal is to answer the questions that help a business grow.

---

## 🧩 What's in the Dataset?

We work with multiple interconnected tables:
- `customers` – who’s placing the order?
- `orders` – when was the order placed?
- `order_items` – what did they buy?
- `products` – details about the items
- `payments` – did the payment go through?

---

## 🛠 SQL Skills Used

- `INNER JOIN`, `LEFT JOIN`
- Aggregations (`SUM`, `AVG`, `COUNT`)
- Filtering with `WHERE` and `HAVING`
- Sorting and grouping
- Multi-table relationships

---

Each file in this folder solves one business question using SQL:
